Japan changes
after dark.

The same streets can feel completely different when the sun disappears. Neon signs wake up, wooden doors close, trains become quieter, and the city slowly reveals another personality.

This gallery is a small collection of those contrasts: modern and traditional, crowded and quiet, bright and almost completely dark.
